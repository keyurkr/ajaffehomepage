New York · Since 1892

Our Heritage

A.JAFFE embodies the art of timeless luxury. Founded in New York in 1892, the brand reflects a legacy shaped by more than 130 years of craftsmanship and expertise. Each ring is hand-finished by master artisans, pursuing perfection from every angle, from the seamless setting of each stone to the signature comfort of our patented designs. The devotion behind every A.JAFFE ring is guaranteed for life, because true craftsmanship endures.
