The A.JAFFE Difference

Crafted in New York Since 1892

A.JAFFE embodies the art of timeless luxury. Founded in New York in 1892, the house reflects a legacy shaped by more than 130 years of craftsmanship and expertise.

Every ring is hand-finished by master artisans, pursuing perfection from every angle — from the seamless setting of each stone to the signature comfort of our patented designs.

The devotion behind every A.JAFFE ring is backed by lifetime service, because true craftsmanship endures.
