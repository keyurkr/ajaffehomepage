Signature Innovation

Engineering You Can Feel

Three A.JAFFE innovations, each solving a real problem of how a ring is worn.

Signature Innovation

Quilts®

A patented quilted pattern worked into the inside of the shank, inspired by 19th-century American quilting.

It cushions the finger for all-day comfort — a private detail only the wearer feels.

See How It Works →

Signature Innovation

Signature Shank®

A squared base at the bottom of the ring — the European shank — that seats the band against your finger.

The ring never spins and the diamond stays upright, with extra gold below for lasting durability.

See How It Works →

Signature Innovation

Expandables™

A band engineered to gently expand, so it slides over the knuckle and settles comfortably into place.

An elegant fit for every hand — comfort and security, without a visible mechanism.

See How It Works →
